The place

A creek for a neighbour

Blue Creek sits at Thanjilode, Meppadi. The cottages are raised among the trees. Below them the water moves over rock, and a thatched pavilion looks out across it.

Enveloped by nature’s symphony, our tranquil resort beckons amidst verdant landscapes, where the melody of a nearby creek serenades guests into a quiet escape.

02 — The water

Take a rejuvenating dip

The pool here is the creek itself. Clear water, flat rock, and the forest doubled on the surface.

River walking

Follow the water upstream

When the creek is gentle, the rocks make a path. A bamboo crossing sits beside the cottages, and the walk can be as short as you like.

03 — While you are here

Summer, rain, and the long evening

The days are for the swing, the water, and a slow walk. The nights are for the pavilion, a tent if you want one, and the fire.

Slow hours on the pavilion

Summer and rain

Bask in the green, or listen to it.

Summer here is shade, a long swing, and the creek within reach. When the rain arrives the forest gets louder, and the pavilion is the place to sit it out. If you walk into the hills, pack light, carry water, and take the path slowly.
